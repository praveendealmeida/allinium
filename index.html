About the Business

Moratuwa-Based. Industry-Focused.

Allinium Fabrication is a fabrication and construction business based at 19/3 Kadalana Road, Moratuwa. We specialise in industrial and commercial building solutions, with work spanning steel fabrication, aluminium fabrication, roofing, and related construction services.

Our customers are business owners, contractors, and facility managers who need fabrication work done properly and communicated clearly. We keep the process simple: understand the requirement, confirm the scope, fabricate to specification, and hand over a finish that does not need rework.

If you have a drawing, a photo, or a site requirement, send it through WhatsApp or call the number below. We will tell you honestly whether we can help and what it will take.
